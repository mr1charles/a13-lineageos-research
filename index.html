Porting LineageOS 23.2 to the Galaxy A13

Research notes from turning a Samsung Galaxy A13 (SM-A135M, Exynos 850) into an Android 16 retro-emulation handheld. Written as a log of what worked, what broke, and why.

Result. Official LineageOS 23.2 system images (built for the Galaxy A21s) boot on the A13 on top of the stock-derived A13 vendor partition, with a patched kernel and adjusted SELinux policy. Emulation (RetroArch, PPSSPP, melonDS) runs with on-screen controls.

Approach

  • Back up every original partition first (super, boot, recovery, dtbo, vbmeta, radio).
  • Flash via TWRP: adb push/dd into /dev/block/mapper/vendor|system and /dev/block/by-name/boot, verifying checksums after each write.
  • Keep the A13 vendor image, replace the system side with the A21s LineageOS build (same SoC family).

Problems and fixes

SELinux policy failed to compile

The vendor sepolicy.cil contained 81 rules from a different OS build that made secilc abort at boot. Removing those rules lets the policy compile; the system runs permissive while the policy is reworked.

Plugging in at power-on boots charger mode

A cable present at boot (or after reboot -p) starts Samsung's offline-charging mode (androidboot.mode=charger). Fixes: make the init on charger trigger run late-init; binary-patch one instruction in the kernel (ldrb w8,[x21,#0xc50] to mov w8,wzr) so the Himax touch driver loads in low-power mode; disable the vendor health charger class.

Force-restart always lands in recovery

The kernel carries a Samsung hard_reset_hook: holding Volume Down + Power for about 7 to 10 seconds triggers a deliberate BUG(), and any panic is redirected to recovery. That is why a forced restart ends in TWRP.

USB gadget switching

The USB gadget HAL cannot switch modes, so adb over USB works only because an early vendor service binds the FunctionFS adb gadget at boot. Wireless debugging works but the Wi-Fi link drops after a few minutes.

Encrypted /data

TWRP cannot read metadata-encrypted /data. First boot needs one twrp format data; wiping with blank ext4 images breaks vold.

RetroArch config unreadable

On Android 16, FUSE rejects access to the app-private external directory, so RetroArch never found its config and the touch overlay never appeared. Placing the config and overlays under the app's data directory fixed it.

Hardware: clamshell handheld

A parametric OpenSCAD clamshell case (phone in the lid, controls in the base) and a 2-layer carrier PCB for an RP2040 and ADS1115 stick reader were designed for the build. The case and PCB are designed but not yet fabricated or tested on hardware.

Limits

  • Exynos 850 is too slow for GameCube-class emulation.
  • SELinux is permissive; a proper policy is unfinished.
  • Kernel patch is a binary edit at a fixed offset and only valid for this exact kernel build.
